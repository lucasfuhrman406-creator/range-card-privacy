Range Card Builder

Privacy policy and support

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Range Card Builder does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Everything you enter (cards, targets, and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you choose to print, save, copy, or share a card, that content goes only where you send it using your device's own print and share features.

Support

Questions, problems, or feature requests: open a support request.

Tips

  • Cards save automatically. Tap Cards to switch, rename, duplicate, or delete them.
  • Turn on Edit Sketch to plot targets by tapping the sketch, or drag them to move.
  • Print opens the share sheet with a US Letter PDF. Tap Print for AirPrint, or Save to Files.

Range Card Builder is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Defense or any military branch. Always verify cards against your unit SOP.
